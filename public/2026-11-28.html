Taiwan votes in tight local elections as China watches

Taiwan's roughly 19.6 million eligible voters chose local leaders across 22 cities and counties and 11,000 posts in Saturday's nine-in-one elections, alongside a nuclear-power referendum. Satellite images show PLA rehearsing civilian-vessel landing bridge in Fujian

Satellite imagery captured a weeks-long PLA amphibious exercise in Dacheng Bay, Fujian, 280km from Taiwan, where eight civilian vessels formed and dismantled an 800-meter floating bridge between Nov 1 and 16, per geospatial firm ingeniSPACE. COO Jason Wang said the PLA appeared to be rehearsing 'low-signature methods to provide logistical support to forces that could be involved in a First Island Chain invasion scenario'; a Taiwan security official confirmed the drill was still underway Nov 27.

Taipei Times; Nov 28

Trump proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners including China

PBOC scraps fixed loan-penalty markup, halves large-deposit threshold
