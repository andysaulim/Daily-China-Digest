Xi Jinping confirms first India trip in nearly seven years for BRICS summit

Huawei racketeering trial opens in Brooklyn weeks before Xi's Washington visit

A federal racketeering conspiracy trial against **Huawei** opened Wednesday in Brooklyn, with DOJ attorney Taylor Stout telling jurors the company stole technology from American firms and helped Iran surveil its citizens over two decades. Defense attorney Brian Heberlig said prosecutors built their case on "a smattering of anecdotes" from Huawei's roughly *200,000 employees* across 170 countries. WSJ notes the trial lands two weeks before Xi's Sept 24 Washington visit.
