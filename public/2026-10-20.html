PRC-Economy

PBOC to Shift Policy-Rate Anchor to Overnight Repo

Pan Gongsheng told the 2026 Financial Street Forum on Oct 19 that the PBOC will study shifting the tenor of its main open-market operation instrument to overnight, making the overnight repo rate the primary policy rate. The central bank separately left the one-year and five-year Loan Prime Rates unchanged for a 17th consecutive month, with the five-year rate holding at 3.5 percent. Bloomberg reported analysts expect the shift to sharpen transmission from the policy rate to market borrowing costs.

The US Department of Homeland Security on Oct 19 ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek-branded products and third-party tools built on its models, citing risk that sensitive data could transfer to China given DeepSeek's PRC-based servers.
