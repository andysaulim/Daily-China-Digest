Fifth Plenum Opens in Beijing With Xi Pressing Party Discipline

US Judge Grants Alibaba Injunction Against Pentagon Blacklist Contract Curbs

A US federal judge on Oct. 26 granted Alibaba a preliminary injunction barring the Pentagon from denying it federal contracts over its placement on a list of firms allegedly aiding China's military. Alibaba sued the US government in June after the designation, which also named Baidu, BYD and other Chinese firms; the company had fired dozens of its Washington lobbyists in response before Monday's ruling.

Taiwan Rejects Beijing's Framing of 1945 'Restoration' Commemorations

Taiwan's government rebuffed Chinese commemorations marking the 81st anniversary of what Beijing calls Taiwan's 1945 'retrocession' from Japan, Reuters reported. Taiwan Affairs Office director Song Tao told a Fuzhou ceremony Oct. 25 that the event was 'an important achievement of China's victory in the War of Resistance against Japan' and reaffirmed Beijing's push for 'national reunification'; Taiwan's Mainland Affairs Council responded only that 'the Republic of China will continue to exist.'
