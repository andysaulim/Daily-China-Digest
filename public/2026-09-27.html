China, US agree to AI-incident channel, $30bn tariff cuts in eight-point summit consensus

The US and China agreed to a bilateral communication channel for AI incidents and more favorable tariff treatment on $30 billion of goods each way, per an eight-point readout jointly published by MOFA, MOFCOM and China's defense ministry after Xi Jinping's September 23-25 Washington state visit. PLA, China Coast Guard run joint Scarborough Shoal drills as Philippines friction mounts

China's Southern Theater Command and China Coast Guard conducted simultaneous joint air-sea drills and 'rights-protection' enforcement exercises around Scarborough Shoal on September 27, days after a coast guard collision near Sabina Shoal on September 18 -- roughly 54 nautical miles from Palawan, the closest such confrontation yet to the Philippine mainland -- and a September 24 resupply standoff at Second Thomas Shoal.
