Russia and China veto US bid to renew UN expert monitoring of Iran sanctions

Moscow and Beijing vetoed the US draft resolution that would have extended the mandate of the independent panel of experts monitoring UN sanctions on Iran, ending outside reporting on enforcement. Both argued the relevant sanctions have expired and the issue should no longer be on the agenda, per the New York Times Chinese edition, which added that Iran is expected to be a major topic at the Trump-Xi meeting.
