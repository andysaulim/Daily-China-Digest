Beijing warns it may cancel Sept. 24 Xi-Trump summit over new Taiwan arms sale

Indo-Pacific

Chinese coast guard expands patrols into Taiwan's Pacific-facing waters

China has deployed an average of two coast guard cutters per month in waters east of Taiwan since June, the first such patrols recorded there since data collection began in January 2025, following Japanese and Philippine plans to define their overlapping maritime border in the region. The patrols covered 27,100 square nautical miles, more than twice Taiwan's land area, while staying 30 nautical miles from Taiwan's coast, per Bloomberg ship-tracking data cited in the report. Vessels carrying oil, gas, iron ore and farm goods made 527 transits through the eastern waters in June, exceeding the 420 that passed through the Taiwan Strait.

Technology

Anthropic says Chinese AI apps secretly routed PLA data to its Claude models

Anthropic disclosed in a 154-page threat-intelligence report that Chinese AI products including Moonshot AI's Kimi and DeepSeek covertly redirected user queries to its Claude system while presenting Claude's outputs as their own, exposing sensitive Chinese and Russian government data to the US company. One case involved a user uploading closed-circuit surveillance footage from around a Chinese military base for behavioral analysis; Anthropic said Moonshot AI rerouted nearly 300,000 customer requests within 10 days via a proxy network of 5,380 fake accounts. Separately, the report said a China-linked user modified electronic-warfare and air-defense simulation software to target 12 Taiwanese sites, including early-warning radar and Patriot/Tien Kung missile positions, before Anthropic banned the account; MOFA spokeswoman Mao Ning said she was not familiar with the specifics and denied any wrongdoing.

German firms boost China investment even as US outlays fall, study finds
