China proposed Friday adding artificial intelligence, biopharmaceutical and photovoltaic manufacturing technologies to its export control list, Reuters and Nikkei Asia reported. The move would extend Beijing's technology-export regime beyond the rare-earth and critical-minerals controls it imposed on the US in October 2025.

Cross-Strait

Chinese coast guard, survey ships swarm Taiwan-controlled waters in multi-point incursion

Two China Coast Guard vessels, numbered 3301 and 3303, entered restricted waters around the Taiwan-controlled Pratas (Dongsha) Islands at 3:34pm Friday; Taiwan's Coast Guard Administration deployed the Cijin patrol ship and patrol boat PP-10032 to shadow and warn them off, citing 11 such incursions this year involving 14 vessel encounters. CGA separately reported 16 Chinese coast guard, survey and fishing vessels operating off Hualien, Pingtung, Kaohsiung and Dongsha the same day, including a survey ship expelled after 12 radio warnings near Eluanbi.

Taipei Times; Liberty Times (自由時報), Oct 18
PRC-Economy

PBOC governor Pan Gongsheng backs tighter IMF scrutiny of advanced-economy fiscal risk

Pan Gongsheng told the IMF's International Monetary and Financial Committee in Bangkok Oct 17 that China supports strengthened IMF surveillance of major developed economies' fiscal risks and spillover effects. He said trade friction and protectionism drag down global growth via supply chains, inflation and disrupted market expectations, urging countries to resist protectionism and support the multilateral trading system.
