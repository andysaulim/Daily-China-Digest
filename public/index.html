PRC-Economy

PBOC's FX Deposit Reserve Ratio Rise to 6% Takes Effect

The increase from 4% to 6% took effect Nov 15; the PBOC last moved the ratio on Sep 15 2023, cutting it from 6% to 4%, and last raised it in December 2021, from 7% to 9%. Minsheng Bank chief economist Wen Bin estimated the two-point rise freezes roughly $24 billion of foreign-currency liquidity against about $1.2 trillion of FX deposits at end-September. The yuan appreciated 4.2% against the dollar in the first nine months and about 0.8% in October, with 14 consecutive sessions of gains from Oct 14.

Cross-Strait

Taiwan Coast Guard Tracks China Coast Guard 2503 After Radio Clash Off Orchid Island

The CGA vessel Hualien detected China Coast Guard ship 2503 at about 3pm Thursday, 40 nautical miles southeast of Orchid Island and 14 nautical miles outside Taiwan's restricted waters; the Chinese vessel broadcast that it was conducting a law enforcement patrol east of Taiwan and that Taiwan is part of China. The Taiwanese vessel replied that the Republic of China (Taiwan) is a sovereign and independent country and demanded the ship leave; by 8am Saturday 2503 was about 67 nautical miles southeast of the Port of Hualien.
