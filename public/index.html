China Confirms First-Ever Xi-Trump-Putin Trilateral for Thursday in Shenzhen

China confirmed Monday that Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin will hold the first-ever face-to-face trilateral between the three countries' leaders on Thursday in Shenzhen, Trump's third face-to-face meeting with Xi this year, per NYT's Chinese edition. MOFA spokesperson Mao Ning called the meeting of 'groundbreaking significance,' saying it will cover safeguarding world peace and the strategic stability of major-power relations. CNBC reports the agenda includes nuclear non-proliferation and the Middle East war; Ukraine and the Jensen Huang espionage case are not expected to come up.

Bessent Gives Nvidia Board 10-Day Deadline Over Alleged China Chip Diversion

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Sunday that Nvidia's board should recruit an outside successor and called for a 'dramatic shakeup' of the company's leadership, questioning whether it ignored signs that advanced chips were rerouted to China through shell companies to inflate revenue, Lianhe Zaobao reported, citing Fox News. Bessent gave Nvidia's board and outside counsel ten days to respond on the Singapore-registered Megaspeed International, which multiple US agencies have pushed to blacklist. House Select Committee on the CCP Chairman John Moolenaar said Nvidia founder Jensen Huang agreed to closed-door testimony on his relationship with former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, with a date still pending.
