●Top Stories
Technology

China drafts export controls on AI, biopharma and solar technologies

The Ministry of Commerce and the Ministry of Science and Technology issued Announcement No. 48 of 2026, opening public comment on a revised Catalogue of Technologies Prohibited or Restricted from Export and applying interim registration management to exports of the technologies concerned. Reuters counted 40 proposed additions across artificial intelligence, biopharmaceuticals and photovoltaics; the FT described the package as aimed at preventing transfer of the most advanced technology overseas. Li Qiang chaired a State Council executive meeting the same day that heard a progress report on the 'AI+' action plan and called for holding the bottom line on AI safety.

Indo-Pacific

Manila protests Chinese ambassador's remark as a thinly veiled threat of force

President Ferdinand Marcos Jr and his government protested remarks by Ambassador Jing Quan, made to reporters on the sidelines of a Manila forum on Thursday after he was asked about Defense Secretary Gilberto Teodoro Jr's description of China as a regional 'troublemaker.' The Department of Foreign Affairs said the wording conveyed a near-naked threat of force and did not help a complex bilateral relationship; Coast Guard spokesman Jay Tarriela called it a direct threat. The Chinese embassy said the DFA had quoted Jing selectively and over-interpreted him, urging Manila to play a more responsible role.

PRC-Economy

Xi inspects green computing hub and wetland reserve in Inner Mongolia

Xi Jinping toured the Chahar High-Tech Development Zone in Ulanqab on Oct 16, meeting computing-centre project managers and technicians and reviewing green computing-power development, then visited the Huangqihai nature reserve in Chahar Right Front Banner.
