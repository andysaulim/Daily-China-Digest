PLA

Taiwan: China Massed Over 100 Vessels in 'Silent Intimidation' Drills

A senior Taiwan security official told the Taipei Times that China conducted an unannounced, large-scale exercise across the western Pacific and around Taiwan in early November, at one point massing more than 100 naval and coast guard vessels and simulating attacks on other militaries and cities including Taiwan's. The official said a Chinese submarine launched an intercontinental ballistic missile from the Yellow Sea onto the mainland during the drills, calling the exercise 'a robber hiding a gun in the pocket of a coat, with the muzzle pointed at the bank teller.' Officials said the drills were larger than 'Justice Mission 2025' a year earlier, one of the largest Chinese naval deployments to date.

Taipei Times, Nov 14, 2026
