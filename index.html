China's Exports Surge 36.3 Percent, Trade Surplus Nears Record

China's exports jumped 36.3 percent year-on-year in September while imports also rose, customs data showed Wednesday, pushing the nine-month trade surplus to $938 billion and putting the full-year total on track to pass 2025's $1.2 trillion record, per AP and Reuters. The Financial Times attributed part of the acceleration to AI-related semiconductor and automobile shipments, calling it the fastest export growth in almost any month since the 2008-09 financial crisis.
