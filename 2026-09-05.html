Xi reportedly plans large Chinese corporate delegation for Washington visit

Xi Jinping plans to bring a large delegation of Chinese corporate executives on his Sept. 24 Washington visit, Reuters reported Sept. 4 citing two sources, an unusual move given Beijing's own multi-year regulatory crackdown on private enterprise and Washington's scrutiny of Chinese investment. One source said the goal is to signal China's willingness to invest in and build commercial ties with the US ahead of the midterms.

So what: Confirms the Sept. 24 Washington summit will be business-centric, raising the odds of new Boeing, agriculture or investment announcements.
