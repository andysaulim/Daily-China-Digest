China triggers 55% tariff on Australian beef as quota threshold reached

Australian beef imports have reached 90 percent of China's annual quota, triggering an additional 55 percent import duty according to the Ministry of Commerce. The tariff adjustment follows the quota system established under bilateral trade agreements. Until recently most Australian beef enjoyed low or zero tariffs under preferential arrangements.

So what: This represents the first major agricultural trade restriction since broader Australia-China trade normalization began in 2023.
