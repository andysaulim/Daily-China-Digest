Technology

Beijing drafts expanded export-control catalogue covering 40 AI, biopharma, solar technologies

China's Ministry of Commerce and Ministry of Science and Technology issued Announcement No. 48 of 2026, seeking public comment on a revised Catalogue of Technologies Prohibited and Restricted from Export covering 40 technologies across AI, biopharma and photovoltaics. Fourteen items are proposed for outright export bans and 26 for restricted export, with AI accounting for 23 of the 40, including large models, agents and compute clusters. Temporary registration management for affected exports takes effect ahead of the formal rule.

US-China

Pentagon designates Confucius Institutes a US national security threat

US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced the Pentagon has designated China-funded Confucius Institutes operating on US campuses as a national security threat, saying the department found 80 new promotional organizations across 30 universities using different names to continue the same mission after Congress banned the institutes years ago. He said the department has 'systematically dismantled every one' of these groups over the past two months, following an October 19 Pentagon announcement that 30 universities ended more than 80 partnerships tied to NDAA Section 1286 list entities.
