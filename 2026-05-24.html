Energy

China coal mine explosion leaves 82 people dead

At least 82 people died in an explosion at a coal mine in Shanxi province, marking China's deadliest mining disaster in over a decade. The blast at the Shanxi Tongzhou Group facility occurred at around 10:30 am local time, with rescue teams working through the night to search for survivors. Local officials accused the mine operators of "serious violations of the law" and immediately ordered all four mines operated by the company to suspend operations.

So what: The disaster tests Xi Jinping's energy security push which has accelerated coal production despite safety concerns.

Taiwan, Chinese coast guard vessels in standoff near Dongsha Island

A Chinese coast guard ship withdrew after a tense standoff with Taiwan forces near Dongsha Island, a Taiwan-controlled feature in the South China Sea. Taiwan's National Security Council head criticized increased Chinese activities in Taiwan's exclusive economic zone. The incident marks the latest in a series of gray-zone operations by Chinese maritime forces around Taiwan-controlled territories.

So what: The Dongsha standoff adds to Cross-Strait tensions ahead of the Trump-Xi summit on May 14-15.
