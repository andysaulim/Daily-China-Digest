China Coal Mine Explosion Kills at Least 82

At least 82 people died in a coal mine explosion in Shanxi province, marking China's deadliest mining disaster since 2009. Xi Jinping ordered all-out rescue efforts while local authorities suspended operations at all four mines operated by Shanxi Tongzhou Group, citing serious legal violations.

So what: The disaster tests limits of Xi's energy security push as China prioritizes coal production amid global energy constraints.
