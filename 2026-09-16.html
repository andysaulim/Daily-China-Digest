PRC-Economy

Hong Kong unveils first-ever five-year plan, aligns with Beijing

Hong Kong Chief Executive John Lee unveiled the city's first-ever five-year plan on Wednesday, aligning local development goals with Beijing's national blueprint for the first time, the Washington Post reported. The nearly three-hour address, Lee's last policy speech of his term, set a target to raise R&D spending to 3 percent of GDP by the early 2030s from 1.63 percent in 2024, per the South China Morning Post. Lee said the blueprint, which centers Hong Kong's role as an offshore yuan hub, was drafted locally with reference to mainland Chinese cities' plans.
