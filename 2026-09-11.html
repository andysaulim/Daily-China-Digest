Technology

Anthropic accuses Alibaba, Moonshot, DeepSeek of covertly training on Claude

Anthropic said it disrupted efforts by China-based AI labs, naming Alibaba, Moonshot AI, DeepSeek and Xiaomi, to feed millions of user conversations with their own models into Claude, then used Claude's responses to distill and improve their systems, CNBC reported. Some intercepted exchanges 'included sensitive information, including from individual users, major multinational companies, and state-affiliated actors,' per Sinocism's summary of the report. MOFA spokesperson Mao Ning rejected the allegations, saying China 'firmly opposes distorting facts to attack and smear China.'

Hong Kong jails Tiananmen vigil leaders Chow, Lee, Ho on subversion charges

A Hong Kong court sentenced Hong Kong Alliance leaders Chow Hang-tung to 7 years 3 months, Lee Cheuk-yan to 7 years, and Albert Ho to 5 years 2 months for 'incitement to subversion' tied to organizing the city's annual Tiananmen vigils, corroborated across NYT, AP and CNA. Judges cited the trio's use of the phrase 'end one-party dictatorship'; all three had been detained since 2021. MOFA said 'relevant parties' should 'respect China's sovereignty and stop making irresponsible remarks' about the case.

Cargo ship fire at Qingdao shipyard kills 25

A fire aboard the Liberian-flagged bulk carrier Ocean Melody, undergoing repairs at China State Shipbuilding Corp's Beihai shipyard in Qingdao, killed at least 25 people and injured five, Chinese state media reported. The blaze broke out around 11:15am Thursday with 42 people aboard and was extinguished by 2:30pm; Xi Jinping ordered a full search-and-rescue effort and 'serious investigation of responsibility,' per Xinhua.
